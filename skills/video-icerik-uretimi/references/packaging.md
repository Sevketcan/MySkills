# Packaging, publishing and conversion

Use this once the video exists: titles, thumbnails, the YouTube description, the CTA, the cuts for each format, and the review after publishing.

## Steps

1. **Title (YT15, YT30).** First sample the niche instead of starting from a universal formula: open videos that already sit in it (one pipeline sets a floor of 15) and record each title, thumbnail text and, where possible, the opening. Write candidates in that niche's language. Don't cite a view count or click-through rate you didn't see, and don't copy an existing title. Write 5-6 candidates. Score them with search data or A/B test them, and pick by the result, not the favourite. Consider treating a tool's predicted optimisation score (for example vidIQ) as a filter for weak candidates; a measured test result or the user's choice makes the decision.
   - One way to source candidates, to test: turn the titles of outlier videos in the niche into fill-in-the-blank patterns (for example "[X], But I Don't Waste Your Time", "What Happens to Your [X] When You [Y]") and adapt them to your topic (YT30).
   - A series can repeat one pattern. A pattern that makes the viewer the subject ("What if you…?") is also worth a test.
   - A title promises only what the video delivers.
2. **Thumbnail (YT15, YT16).** Produce several candidates and test them. One approach to test: a thumbnail that tells the story before the click (before → after).
3. **YouTube description (YT17).** One format to try: the keyword in the first 2 lines, timestamped chapters, at most 3 hashtags. If a model wrote it, consider the writing-tic pass from [script-and-hook.md](script-and-hook.md), step 5 (YT27).
4. **CTA and conversion (YT18).** Turn comments into leads: "comment KEYWORD and I'll send you the link", followed by an automatic DM.
   - The user approves the setup.
   - Check the platform's rules on automated messages first.
   - On an affiliate channel, consider placing the affiliate call in the first third of the video (YT24).
5. **Cuts (VA10, YT19).**
   - Designed motion: consider a separate composition for each format rather than a crop (VA10).
   - Camera footage: crop to 9:16 framed on the face or on the screen being shown, not from the centre (VA10). Editing the footage itself: [talking-edit.md](talking-edit.md).
   - Consider producing separate 9:16 and 16:9 cuts from one raw take (YT19).
6. **After publishing, carry the data into the next video (YT26).** Use the signals the platform gives:
   - the retention curve: one approach to test is to export it, have the model find where and why viewers drop, and change that part in the next script;
   - average view duration against length: if viewers leave well before the end of a short, shorten it or tighten the opening next time (YT22);
   - the share of views from non-followers, saves and shares, which show whether the video reached new viewers.

   Report measured values separately from interpretations.

## Notes

- Upload as private by default; publishing and DM automation need the user's approval.
- If an agent drafts the packaging, consider giving it read access and a single job folder only, with no upload, live-title or comment tools; run a separate reviewer pass before anything is published, with at most one retry (VA15, YT32).
- Don't invent view counts or expected results for a title or thumbnail. Report what was measured and what is an estimate.
