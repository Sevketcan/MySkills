# Packaging, publishing and conversion

Use this once the video exists: titles, thumbnails, the YouTube description, the CTA and the cuts for each format.

## Steps

1. **Title (K28).** Write 5-6 candidates. Score them with search data or A/B test them, and pick the highest score, not the favourite.
2. **Thumbnail (K28, K29).** Produce several candidates and test them. One approach to test: a thumbnail that tells the story before the click (before → after).
3. **YouTube description (K30).** One format to try: the keyword in the first 2 lines, timestamped chapters, at most 3 hashtags.
4. **CTA and conversion (K31).** A pattern to test: "comment KEYWORD and I'll send you the link", followed by an automatic DM.
   - The user approves the setup.
   - Check the platform's rules on automated messages first.
5. **Cuts (K24, K32).** Consider producing separate 9:16 and 16:9 compositions from one raw take rather than deriving the vertical version by cropping.

## Notes

- Upload as private by default; publishing and DM automation need the user's approval.
- Don't invent view counts or expected results for a title or thumbnail. Report what was measured and what is an estimate.
