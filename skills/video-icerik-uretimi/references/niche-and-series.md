# Niche, topics and series

Use this when a channel is going to be started: validate the niche, find topics and turn a working format into a series.

## Steps

1. **Validate the niche (K7).** Check:
   - Do small channels in the niche get outlier views (demand exceeds supply)?
   - Is RPM around $5 or higher? This is an estimate; say where the figure comes from.
   - Can you list at least 100 video ideas?
   - Can it be told without showing a face?

   State every criterion you could not verify. Use these thresholds as a first filter, not as a fixed rule.
2. **Pull topics from demand signals (K8):**
   - Reddit questions with many comments;
   - trending and outlier videos (see [reference-analysis.md](reference-analysis.md));
   - Google Trends.
3. **Turn a working format into a series and put it on a calendar (K9).**
   - The calendar's job is to make sure no upload is missed.
   - One example cadence is two videos a week for 15 weeks. Treat it as an example to adapt, not a target.

## Notes

- Never promise earnings or views from a niche. Income figures in sources are cases, not rules.
- Producing the videos: [faceless-pipeline.md](faceless-pipeline.md) for faceless channels, [script-and-hook.md](script-and-hook.md) for short-form scripts.
