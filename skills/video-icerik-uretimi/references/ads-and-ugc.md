# Ad and UGC variants

Use this to rebuild a winning ad structure with your own product and to run a UGC programme. Ad budgets, MMP and ROAS are out of scope (see Boundaries in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md)).

## Steps

1. **Extract the structure** of winning ads with [reference-analysis.md](reference-analysis.md) (K33).
2. **Rebuild it with your own product and real outputs.**
   - Produce 3-5 variants by swapping parts: hook, presenter, text, product.
   - One approach to test: tie the timing to words, so the edit holds when the text changes (K27).
3. **UGC programme.**
   - The creator sends a raw draft.
   - An editing pipeline (an agent) edits and checks it.
   - Weekly views decide whom to continue with.

## Notes

- Don't suggest publishing ad-like UGC without a label, or building hooks on shock content.
- Take only the structure of someone else's ad; never their footage, faces, voice, text or logo.
- Paid generation for variants needs a cost estimate and the user's approval first.
