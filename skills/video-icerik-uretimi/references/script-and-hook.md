# Script and hook: 30-60 second short-form video

Use this for Reels, Shorts and TikTok scripts, or for the opening of a longer video. If there is a reference video or ad, break it down first with [reference-analysis.md](reference-analysis.md) and build on its structure (K5).

## Steps

1. **Stakes (K1).** Set up:
   - a character the viewer sides with (the user, a customer, you);
   - something they could lose (money, time, an opportunity);
   - a deadline.

   Make all three clear in the first 2-3 seconds.
2. **Opening pattern (K4).** Pick one of these openings to test:
   - cold open: drop the viewer into someone's moment;
   - show the object: the result in hand, then a surprise;
   - "you" view: a familiar situation;
   - bold statement: one sentence that sounds wrong;
   - personal confession;
   - raw footage.

   Don't open with "Hi, in this video…" (K1).
3. **Loop (K2, K3).** stakes → big question → twist → re-hook.
   - **Big question:** don't ask it outright; leave the information incomplete. Keep to one question and delay the answer (K2).
   - **Twist:** "everyone thinks ___, actually ___". It must not contradict what was said at the start.
   - **Re-hook:** "but that wasn't the real problem".
   - **Rhythm:** a starting point to test is 2-3 loops in 45-60 seconds, a new curiosity roughly every 15-20 seconds (K3).
4. **Check.**
   - Is it clear within 3 seconds who could lose what?
   - Is there one clear question in the viewer's head?
   - Is the twist consistent with the opening?
   - Does it flow when read aloud?
5. **Output (K6).**
   - The script, with the step it serves next to each sentence.
   - Three alternative opening lines.
   - The script's weakest point.
   - Ask the user to add their own example and figures; the first draft is only a starting point. A human checks the hook and every factual claim before publishing.
