# Rejection response

1. **Map the guideline** in the rejection message to the check list in [pre-submission.md](pre-submission.md):
   - 5.1.1(v) → section A (account deletion).
   - 2.1 (incomplete app) → the reviewer notes and demo steps (K3).
   - 3.1.2 → how the billed amount is presented (K6).
   - 5.6 → fake urgency or a fake discount (K6).
   - 4.3 → a copy or spam appearance (K8).
2. **A very fast rejection** (seconds, according to the source) may mean the build was caught by automated screening. Adding a video won't fix it; make the app more original (K8). This is a claim, not a verified fact.
3. **If the same reason repeats,** consider requesting an App Review appointment (K10):
   1. Go to developer.apple.com/events/view/upcoming-events and search for "app review".
   2. Pick an "App Review appointment" on a suitable date. Sessions fill quickly and requests have a deadline.
   3. In the request, give the app's Apple ID, the rejection text, the guideline number and the issue itself, plus the preferred language. An active Developer Program membership is required.
4. **Describe the fix step by step** in the reviewer notes, then resubmit.
5. **Account terminated under 3.2(f):** consider appealing to Apple Support, and check App Store Connect regularly; a reinstatement may not be announced by email (K11).
