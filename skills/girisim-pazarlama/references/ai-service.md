# Positioning an AI service or agent product

Use this when the product is an AI service, consulting offer or agent that does work for a business.

- **Problem:** pick one expensive, recurring business problem. Consider validating demand with a paid pilot (G18).
- **Scope:** the agent does that one job end to end, with human approval at the critical step (G17).
- **Sales line:** sell the outcome, not the feature: "this process gets this much shorter, like this", in time, cost or errors (G16).
- **Several agents:** if the work is split across agents, consider a brief that states the goal, the delivery format, the roles and the verification criterion (G19).
