# Channel plan: which channel now, and in what order

Use this when the question is where the next customers should come from.

## Steps

1. **Measure today's channels.** For each one, record what it brings from analytics, referrers or the signup source. Mark missing data as missing.
2. **Pick one channel for the current stage (G1, G2).** The ladder below is one ordering, with stage thresholds, that worked for a B2B SaaS product. Treat it as a starting hypothesis and adapt it to the user's product: for a consumer product the first channel may be community or search.
   - **0-100 customers:** reach people who show intent directly (outbound), or answer intent questions in communities (G7). See [intent-posts.md](intent-posts.md).
   - **Early, and the product works:** share value on Reddit and in communities (G4); SEO (`seo-ai-arama`).
   - **Trust is needed:** content plus a free template or blueprint; founder-led content.
   - **Partnership stage:** influencers, newsletter sponsorships, long-running affiliate programs (G5).
   - **Paid ads last,** once unit economics hold (G3). Run [offer-and-ads.md](offer-and-ads.md) before any ad spend.
3. **Write a 4-week criterion** for the chosen channel: what result counts as "working".
4. **When it works, add the next channel on top** and keep the working one running (G1).

## Channel notes

- **Reddit and communities (G4):** cheap and strong early, but only when sharing value: an educational analysis, a changelog of real features, an answer to a question that shows intent.
- **Affiliate (G5):** long-running terms (recurring or lifetime commission, a long cookie) give leverage, and affiliates' posts also bring backlinks.
