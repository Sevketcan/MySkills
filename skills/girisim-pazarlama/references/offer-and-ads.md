# Offer and ads check

Use this before recommending ads or more traffic, and when traffic arrives but money doesn't.

## 1. Offer (G10)

Ask for a one-sentence answer to each question:

- Why us?
- Why now?
- How are we different from the competitor?

If an answer is missing, don't recommend ads. Work on the offer first.

## 2. Leaking bucket (G10)

1. Measure the rate from visitor to target action (signup, trial, purchase, click to the store) per page type.
2. Fix the weakest step before adding traffic.

## 3. If ads are running (G11)

Consider testing whether ads bring new customers rather than existing ones:

- exclude existing customers in one ad set and compare;
- report the cost per new customer separately.

## 4. Content and UGC reports (G12)

For high-volume content or UGC, consider reporting the floor (the lowest day) instead of the peak.

## Guardrails

- Opening an ad budget and changing prices need the user's approval.
- Never invent conversion or revenue numbers; mark missing data as missing.
