# Turkish tax questions: 20/B, Bağ-Kur and VAT on foreign services

Information only, not advice (G21, G36). Relay what the sources say, list the open questions, and tell the user to verify everything with a mali müşavir (certified accountant) before acting. Figures are 2026 values as the sources report them; check the current ones.

## How the 20/B exemption works, according to the sources (verify with an expert)

1. Obtain the exemption certificate (istisna belgesi).
2. Open a dedicated bank account.
3. Route every payment into that account.
4. A 15% withholding (stopaj) is the final tax.
5. The 2026 limit is reported as 5.3 million TL.

## Bağ-Kur (verify with an expert)

- If the person has no other SGK coverage, Bağ-Kur reportedly starts with the first payment.
- While income is small, applying early can turn the earnings negative.

## VAT on foreign software and AI subscriptions (G36, verify with an expert)

What the source describes in its corrected account (its first version said no VAT is due; the correction says the VAT moves to the buyer). Verify it with a mali müşavir:

- Buying a foreign subscription as a business, with a tax number, reportedly gets an invoice without VAT. The VAT doesn't disappear: the buyer declares and pays it on the KDV-2 return ("sorumlu sıfatıyla").
- A VAT-registered buyer can then deduct it on the KDV-1 return. The total cost stays the same; the gain is the right to deduct.
- A buyer who is not VAT-registered gains nothing and takes on the filing.
- Not declaring it reportedly risks a tax-loss penalty (vergi ziyaı cezası).

Don't tell the user whether this route suits them. Hand it to the accountant as a question.
