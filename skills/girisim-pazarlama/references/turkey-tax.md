# Turkish tax question: the 20/B exemption and Bağ-Kur

Information only, not advice (G21). Relay what the sources say, list the open questions, and tell the user to verify everything with a mali müşavir (certified accountant) before acting. Figures are 2026 values as the sources report them; check the current ones.

## How the exemption works, according to the sources (verify with an expert)

1. Obtain the exemption certificate (istisna belgesi).
2. Open a dedicated bank account.
3. Route every payment into that account.
4. A 15% withholding (stopaj) is the final tax.
5. The 2026 limit is reported as 5.3 million TL.

## Bağ-Kur (verify with an expert)

- If the person has no other SGK coverage, Bağ-Kur reportedly starts with the first payment.
- While income is small, applying early can turn the earnings negative.

## Open questions for the accountant
